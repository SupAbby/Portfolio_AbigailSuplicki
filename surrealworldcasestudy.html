SURREAL WORLD

// ROLE Motion Graphics Designer
// DURATION 6 Weeks
// CLIENT School Project
// TOOLS After Effects, Procreate, Photoshop
SYS_ID: SURREAL_WORLD MOTION GRAPHICS
STATUS: COMPLETE NOVEMBER 2025

// PROJECT OVERVIEW

The project aimed to create a visually complex animation of what could be considered a surreal world. I utilized various motion graphics techniques to bring this imaginative concept to life. I created many unique frame-by-frame animation in procreate and edited the image of the person in photoshop. I aligned, timed, and animated the pieces togther in after effects. This project tought me many different ways to work with after effects and how to use the tools to create a more complex animation. I also learned how to use procreate to create frame-by-frame animation and developed my skills in editing images in photoshop.
