SNAKES

// ROLE UI/UX Designer
// DURATION 6 Weeks
// CLIENT School Project
// TOOLS Figma, Cinema 4D, Illustrator, Procreate, After Effects
SYS_ID: SNAKES_WEB WEBSITE DESIGN
STATUS: COMPLETE MAY 2026

// PROJECT OVERVIEW

PROBLEMS:

Snakes are often seen as scary or evil in media, but your common pet snake, such as a corn snake, is significantly less dangerous than a dog. I want more people to be able to learn about snakes and the ability to have them as wonderful pets rather than fear them.
While many people do research before purchasing a snake, informational sites can have very lengthy paragraphs that many users will just skip over. This leads to missing important information or a user potentially giving up.

GOALS:

Introduce users to a variety of beginner friendly snake species while educating them on different aspects of the snake and what is needed to keep them as a pet.
Keeping the user engaged though interactive elements and graphics while still delivering the needed information.

MOTIVATION:

I believe snakes are very beautiful and unique animals. I have had a love for keeping reptiles since I was young and I want to share the experience. The fear of snakes has become a rather common thing, but I believe if people are able to learn more about snakes this fear can be reduced.

PROCESS:

For this project, I conducted extensive research on snake care and behavior to ensure the information provided was accurate and helpful. I then created wireframes and prototypes to visualize the user experience and iterated on the design based on feedback. I used Cinema 4D, zbrush, and Adobe Substance Painter to create the 3D assents for the website. I created a background asset using procreate then designed the UI in Figma. Once I had a final composition, I rendered from cinema 4D and prototyped in Figma. Using the prototype, I created recordings and pieced them together to make a final video to showcase the project.
